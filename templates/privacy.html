Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use SecureScan Pro, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Scan Data: URLs you submit for scanning and the resulting security assessment data.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, scan frequency, and browser type for analytics purposes.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our security scanning services
  • To generate security reports and compliance documents
  • To send scan completion notifications and monitoring alerts
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To communicate important service updates

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit
  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing with unique salts
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Scan results are automatically deleted after 90 days unless saved by authenticated users

4. Scan Privacy

All security scans are performed privately. We do not:

  • Share scan results with third parties
  • Publish or index scan data
  • Use scan data for marketing purposes
  • Store credentials submitted for authenticated scans beyond the scan session

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party APIs (NVD, Shodan, VirusTotal) to enhance scan results. Only the target URL or hostname is shared with these services, never your personal data or credentials.

6. Data Retention

  • Free tier scan results: 7 days
  • Paid plan scan results: 1 year or until account deletion
  • Account data: Until you request deletion

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request data correction or deletion
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or share cookie data with advertisers.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: privacy@securescanpro.com
