Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied with SecureScan Pro within 14 days of your initial purchase, contact us for a full refund.

2. Eligibility

  • Refund requests must be made within 14 days of the initial subscription purchase
  • The guarantee applies to first-time subscribers only
  • Annual plan refunds are prorated after the 14-day period

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Email billing@securescanpro.com with your account email and reason for refund
  • We will process your refund within 5-10 business days
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • Professional services (penetration testing, security audits, consulting) once work has begun
  • Custom enterprise agreements after deployment
  • Refund requests made after 14 days (monthly plans)

5. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Upon cancellation:

  • Your access continues until the end of the current billing period
  • No further charges will be made
  • Your scan history remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation
  • You can resubscribe at any time

6. Annual Plan Refunds

For annual plans cancelled after the 14-day guarantee period:

  • Refunds are prorated based on remaining months
  • A 10% early termination fee applies
  • Refund = (Remaining months / 12) x Annual price - 10% fee

7. Contact

For billing questions: billing@securescanpro.com
